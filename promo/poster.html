What you'll do

  • Pitch to investors. Candid VC office hours on what makes a company fundable.
  • Meet your future customers. Design partners, pricing and pilots.
  • Learn from alumni founders who built companies from zero to scale.
  • Hang out with industry leaders in AI, robotics and deep tech.
  • Pitch Day. Present to a panel of VCs. The winning startup takes the $50,000 award.

Who & when

Open to current MIT undergraduates and MEng students. Any stage, from a developed concept to a company with customers. Grants cover travel and shared lodging.
